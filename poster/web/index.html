Flight Dose Lab

At cruising altitude, cosmic rays deliver a radiation dose that changes with where you fly and what the Sun is doing. A physics model, NAIRAS, estimates it everywhere. Here you can see where it misses, and how neural networks learn to correct it.

How attention reads a storm

A real flight over Florida on 9 September 2017, the morning after a strong geomagnetic storm. Each column is one token: the space-weather readings at one moment. Attention decides which moments matter.

The attention weights here come from a hand-set query that looks for storm conditions. They show the mechanism; the trained model learns its own queries.
